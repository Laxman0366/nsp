Our origin

From emergency relief to long-term community development

The 1978 cyclone, the prolonged drought, and the 1980 flood created a humanitarian crisis across Odisha. Volunteers inspired by Gandhian values mobilized donors, delivered relief, and eventually shaped that response into Nilachal Seva Pratisthan.

NSP has continued to expand from immediate support into structured work in social, economic, environmental, and human-resource development.

What we stand for

Service, empathy, and dignity

  • Service to people first, especially those at the margins.
  • Compassion that turns relief into rehabilitation.
  • Programs designed to build confidence and opportunity.
  • Accountability that keeps the mission credible and open.
