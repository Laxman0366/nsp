Message

I am pleased to share NSP’s journey of care, resilience, and service. What began as an urgent response to suffering has matured into a centre of consistent support for vulnerable children, women, persons with disabilities, older adults, and families in distress.

Our work has never been about one intervention alone. We continue to combine shelter, food, education, health care, counseling, awareness, and rehabilitation so that people can move from dependence toward dignity and participation.

None of this is possible without the support of our staff, partners, donors, volunteers, and governing members. Their commitment keeps the organization accountable and mission-driven.

Subas Chandra Gajendra

Founder-cum-Secretary, Nilachal Seva Pratisthan

A steady commitment to equal opportunity, rehabilitation, and human dignity.
