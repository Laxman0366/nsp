About Organization

A community organization shaped by crisis response and continuity.

NSP grew from volunteer relief into a structured social welfare organization. Its work now spans child welfare, women’s support, disability care, education, rehabilitation, and awareness-led community development.

Vision

A society built on dignity, health, learning, and equal opportunity.

In our envisioned future, we see a society that stands as a beacon of hope and progress, characterized by its unwavering commitment to the well-being of its citizens. This society places paramount importance on health, ensuring that every individual enjoys access to quality healthcare, both as a fundamental right and a cornerstone of human dignity. Education is the bedrock upon which this vision is built, fostering intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and a love for lifelong learning. It is a system that leaves no one behind, equipping every person with the knowledge and skills they need to not only survive, but to thrive in an ever-evolving world.

Empowerment is woven into the fabric of this society, breaking down barriers and amplifying the voices of the marginalized. It is a place where individuals are not only given the tools to succeed, but are also encouraged to dream beyond the confines of their circumstances. Through inclusive policies and support networks, we forge a path towards self-reliance, enabling each person to shape their destiny and contribute meaningfully to the collective progress of society.

Mission

Grassroots empowerment through care, capacity, and self-reliance.

Our mission is dedicated to uplifting individuals at the grassroots level by focusing on enhancing their capacities and addressing their diverse needs and challenges. This empowerment is achieved through both individual and collective efforts, recognizing that supporting communities as a whole is just as important as aiding individuals. The ultimate goal is to foster self-sufficiency, allowing people to become self-reliant and independent.

Central to our mission is the belief in ensuring the well-being and dignity of every individual. This involves not only addressing immediate needs but also promoting long-term health and prosperity. It also emphasizes equality, self-respect, and the support systems people need to thrive and contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Objectives

Practical interventions that turn commitment into structured support.

Our objectives center around implementing scalable interventions in collaboration with government and various agencies, demonstrating a comprehensive approach to community development.

  • Skill enhancement and capacity-building for better economic opportunity.
  • Disability rehabilitation, care, and inclusive support systems.
  • Women’s empowerment, rehabilitation, and social defense.
  • Child care, rehabilitation, and dignified support for older persons.

Target Population

Focused on people who face exclusion, vulnerability, or neglect.

Our organization is a beacon of hope and support for a diverse range of individuals facing various challenges in society. From persons with disabilities to vulnerable children, destitute women, and senior citizens, NSP extends a caring hand to those who need it most.

The outreach also includes youth, people affected by alcoholism or drug abuse, and other disadvantaged groups who need protection, rehabilitation, and a stronger pathway back into community life.

How we work

Grounded in local partnerships

NSP works with families, institutions, volunteers, and public systems to create responses that are practical, humane, and sustainable.

Core approach

  • Relief and rehabilitation for vulnerable groups.
  • Skill-building, care services, and awareness campaigns.
  • Programs that connect dignity with opportunity.

Community reach

From Puri district outward

The organization operates from Benagaon in Kanas block and serves people across Odisha through targeted, need-based programs.
